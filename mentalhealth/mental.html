Anxiety

Anxiety is more than just feeling "stressed out" before a big presentation; it is the body’s internal alarm system stuck in the "on" position. While occasional worry is a natural human response, an anxiety disorder involves persistent, excessive fear that feels disproportionate to the actual situation and interferes with daily functioning. Physically, it often manifests as a racing heart, shallow breathing, or muscle tension—the "fight-or-flight" response being triggered even when no immediate danger is present. Factually, anxiety disorders are the most common mental health conditions worldwide, affecting roughly 19% of adults in the United States alone. It is important to remember that anxiety is not a character flaw, but a highly treatable physiological condition; with the right tools, like therapy or mindfulness, you can essentially "recalibrate" a brain that is being a bit too overprotective

Eating disorders

Eating disorders are complex mental and physical illnesses that involve a persistent disturbance in eating behaviors and distressing thoughts or emotions regarding food, body weight, and shape. They are not a "lifestyle choice," a "phase," or a result of vanity. Instead, they often serve as a coping mechanism for underlying psychological issues such as trauma, anxiety, or a need for control. These conditions can affect anyone, regardless of age, gender, ethnicity, or body size, and they can have devastating effects on every organ system in the human body
