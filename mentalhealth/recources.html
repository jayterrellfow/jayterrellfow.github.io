The NAMI Blog

Best for: Personal stories and advocacy. The National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI) features posts from individuals living with mental health conditions, their family members, and health professionals. It’s a great place to find relatable content on topics like navigating the holidays, managing specific diagnoses, and fighting stigma The term "blog" is a linguistic shorthand that evolved from the phrase "weblog," which was originally coined by Jorn Barger in 1997 to describe the process of "logging the web." In 1999, programmer Peter Merholz playfully broke the word into the phrase "we blog" in his sidebar, and the shortened version stuck. Conceptually, a blog is a dynamic website or section of a site where content—known as "posts"—is displayed in reverse-chronological order (newest first). While they began as simple online diaries or personal journals, blogs have matured into essential tools for journalism, corporate marketing, and niche community-building, characterized by their informal tone and the ability for readers to interact through comments.
